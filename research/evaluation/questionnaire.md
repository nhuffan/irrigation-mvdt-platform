# Custom usability/usefulness questionnaire

Not SUS. Use participant pseudonym and dataset version; retain consent and identity mapping separately. Administer after tasks without coaching responses.

Scale: 1 Strongly disagree · 2 Disagree · 3 Neutral · 4 Agree · 5 Strongly agree. NA = not experienced/not applicable; blank = no response.

1. Asset information was easy to locate.
2. GIS representation helped me understand asset location.
3. O&M history was easy to follow.
4. Source/document linkage improved information traceability.
5. 3D representation helped me understand the asset context.
6. The system could support routine O&M information retrieval.
7. The interface was understandable.
8. I would prefer this workflow over searching fragmented files for similar tasks.

Open questions:

- What information was still difficult to find?
- Which feature was most useful?
- Which O&M information is missing?
- Would 3D be useful in routine work? When?
- What should be improved before operational deployment?

Store responses in a copy of `templates/usability_observations.csv` under `observations/`. Each row needs an evidence reference. Quote comments only with appropriate participant consent and pseudonymous attribution. No questionnaire results have been collected by this implementation.
